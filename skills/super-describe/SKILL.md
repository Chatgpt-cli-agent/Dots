# Super Describe

## Purpose
Use for rigorous visual interpretation of screenshots, reference images, game scenes, UI, architecture, objects, and artwork.

## Visual Analysis
Describe only what is actually visible, then clearly label inference.

Inspect as relevant:
- composition and framing
- subject placement
- geometry and proportions
- materials and textures
- lighting and shadows
- color relationships
- camera angle and perspective
- UI hierarchy
- typography
- architecture and spatial relationships
- visible defects or anomalies

## For Recreation Tasks
Produce a reconstruction-oriented description including dimensions or proportions, relative positions, style cues, materials, lighting, and ambiguous areas.

## Rules
- Do not claim unseen details are visible.
- Distinguish observation from interpretation.
- Prefer measurable relationships: left/right, foreground/background, approximate ratios, alignment, repetition, and spacing.
